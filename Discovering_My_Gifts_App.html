Discovering My Gift(s)

Find where you fit in the Body of Christ

This tool walks you through 124 short statements to help you discover your spiritual gifts, based on the Gift Guide: Spiritual Gifts for Church Growth, published by the National Christian Education Directorate, Foursquare Gospel Church of Nigeria — freely shared for any church or fellowship to use.

For each statement, you'll choose how true it has been of your life:
Much (3) · Some (2) · Little (1) · Not at all (0)

At the end, you'll see your dominant gifts (top 3), your subordinate gifts (next 3), how they compare with what you currently serve in, and a card you can share.
